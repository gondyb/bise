bise for desktop :* · v0 · a proposal for the user

every feature of the terminal app, in a window of its own. same product, same words, same marks. the terminal taught us what matters; the window only adds what a terminal can't do well: real clicks, images inline, a palette, notifications, a place in the menu bar.

  • one thread per repo. the sidebar lists your repos like chats. picking one opens its thread with main. there's no "new chat": a repo is the thread, forever (compaction keeps it short).
  • you still talk to main. the agents live in the panel on the right. click one to talk to it directly, esc to come back.
  • a sans to read, mono where it means something. a sans for everything you read (the thread, the sidebar, settings): Geist (the user's pick); the buttons below swap it to compare. JetBrains Mono for the wordmark, the marks (:* ∿ ✓ ? ψ ✉ $ ƒ), keys, code, paths and commands. in the history, the sans is someone talking to you (you, bise, a report) and the mono is the machine at work (calls, folds, messages between agents): it reads like a log, and you skip it without trying. the same palette, lowercase. "mono everywhere" is there to compare.
  • quiet by default. calls fold into one row, messages between agents stay small, what needs you waits in the inbox above the composer. ⌘E opens everything.
  • keys feel like a mac app. every shortcut is a ⌘ key (the user: « utilisons commande pour plus de choses »): no ctrl, no ⌥ to learn. hold ⌘ to see them where they act.
  • closing the window doesn't stop the work. agents keep running; bise stays in the menu bar and tells you when something needs you.
