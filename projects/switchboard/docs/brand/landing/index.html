why

    
a day of coding with agents feels empty.

    
you started five things. you watched output scroll. you answered "should i continue?". you checked which terminal was doing what.

    
you never got to think. something always pulled you out.

    
that's not you. that's no flow.

    
bise protects your flow. you talk, whenever, however. i run the agents in the background, answer the obvious questions myself, and only tap your shoulder when it matters.

  







  

    
how

    
you talk. i run the agents.
you stay in flow.

    
    
      
  • 1say it, and keep going.mid-thought, as often as you want. no prompt to craft, no workflow to design. i'm always listening.
    • 
      
  • 2i start the agents.one per thing, in the background, in your folder. they know about each other and sort things out quietly.
    • 
      
  • 3you only hear what matters.real decisions come to you as a card. when something ships, you get a ♡.
